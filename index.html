Modulo ADC Lab

Conventional ADCs clip when a signal exceeds their range. A modulo ADC folds it back instead, and the original is recovered digitally. We study modulo sampling from theory and algorithms to FPGA hardware and applications.

Research

Theory

Multi-channel modulo ADCs, robust CRT and bit-depth analysis.

Algorithms

Low-rate, noise-robust signal reconstruction.

Hardware

FPGA-calibrated prototype with over 100× dynamic-range extension.

Applications

Radar imaging, mmWave communications and speech.

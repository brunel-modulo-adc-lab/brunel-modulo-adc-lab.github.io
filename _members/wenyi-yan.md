Wenyi Yan received the B.S. degree in communication engineering from Chongqing University of Posts and Telecommunications, Chongqing, China, in 2022.

She is currently working toward the Ph.D. degree at Brunel University of London, London, U.K. Her current research interests include the acquisition of high dynamic range signals using modulo ADCs and the associated hardware and algorithm design.

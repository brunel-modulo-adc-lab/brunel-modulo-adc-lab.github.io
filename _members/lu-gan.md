Dr Lu Gan is a Reader in Signal and Image Processing in the College of Engineering, Design and Physical Sciences, Brunel University of London.
She received her B.Eng. and M.Eng. degrees from Southeast University, China, in 1998 and 2000, and her Ph.D. from Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, in 2004.
Before joining Brunel in 2008, she held faculty positions at the University of Newcastle, Australia, and the University of Liverpool, UK.

Her research covers fundamental signal processing theory and its applications, including modulo sampling and high-dynamic-range ADCs, compressed sensing and structured sparse signal processing, multirate signal processing, image and video processing, non-destructive testing, sparse antenna arrays and wireless communications.
